track_changes How Auto-Tracking Works

DnDTools listens to the game's network traffic in real time using TShark (packet capture). When you open a merchant, accept a quest, or complete objectives in Dark and Darker, the app intercepts those packets and extracts quest data automatically.

The captured data is reconciled with the DarkerDB quest database to match quest IDs, objectives, and item requirements. This allows the tracker to:

  • Detect which quests you have accepted, completed, or haven't picked up yet
  • Track individual objective progress (e.g. "Bring 3 Healing Potions — 2/3")
  • Show live merchant status in the gallery cards

Your captured quest status (accepted, in-progress, completed) is saved across sessions so you won't lose progress when you close the app.

Requirements: WireShark must be installed and packet capture must be enabled in Settings for auto-tracking to function.
